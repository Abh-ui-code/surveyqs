# Agent guide — the mobile app

> **Audience & scope.** Field agents who conduct interviews. Plain language, in the order you will actually do things. Everything here happens on your phone. Nothing in this guide requires an internet connection unless it says so.

## What the app does

Your supervisor assigns you surveys. You open one, interview someone, and submit. The app **works completely offline** — you can interview all day with no signal, and everything sends itself when you next have a connection.

You see only the surveys assigned to you, and only the responses you collected.

---

## Signing in

The first time, enter the email and password your administrator gave you. You will be asked to set your own password.

After that, if your phone supports it, turn on fingerprint or face unlock in **Profile → Biometric unlock**. You will not need your password again on this phone.

**You need a connection once, to sign in.** After that you can work offline for as long as you need to.

---

## Before you go out — the ten-minute check

Do this while you still have signal. It is the difference between a productive day and a wasted one.

1. Open the app and pull down on the Surveys screen to refresh.
2. Every survey you need should say **"Form up to date"**.
3. If one says **"New version available"** or **"Not downloaded"**, tap **Update** or **Download** now.
4. Check the Sync tab is empty or shows only "Recently synced".
5. Check your battery and your storage.

A survey you have not downloaded cannot be started in a village with no signal.

## Doing an interview

### 1. Start

Tap **Start** on the survey. You will see the title, roughly how long it takes, what you will need — a photo, a location reading — and any instructions from your supervisor.

### 2. Record who you are speaking to

The app asks for their **phone number first**. As you type it, if this person has been interviewed before, the app will tell you:

```
⚠ This person may already exist
  Ramesh Patil · Jainad
  Last interviewed 4 Mar 2026
  [ Use this person ]   [ New person ]
```

Tap **Use this person** if it is them. Their details fill in and you skip the typing. This also keeps your organisation's records clean.

### 3. Read the consent notice

The app shows the notice in your chosen language. **Read it aloud.** It tells the person what will be collected and why.

- If they agree, tap the confirm button — or collect a signature, depending on what your survey requires.
- If they decline, tap **Declined**. The interview ends and **nothing is saved**, not even the name you typed.

This step is not optional and is not a formality. It is what makes the rest of the interview lawful.

### 4. Ask the questions

One section per screen. A progress bar shows where you are.

- Questions marked with a red `*` must be answered.
- Some questions appear and disappear as you answer — that is intended. The survey only asks what applies.
- Tap **Next** to move on. If something is missing or out of range, the app tells you which question and why, right there.
- **‹ Back** returns to the previous screen. Your answers are kept.

### 5. Photos, locations and scans

- **Photo** — tap, take the picture, review it, keep or retake. Take it before you move on; going back is slower than getting it right.
- **Location** — tap Capture. The app shows how accurate the reading is and turns green when it is good enough. Standing outside, away from walls, helps. If it will not settle, wait ten seconds rather than accepting a poor fix.
- **Barcode** — point the camera. If the label is damaged, tap **Enter manually**.

### 6. Check and submit

The last screen lists everything you recorded. Tap any answer to jump back and fix it. Questions the survey skipped are shown greyed out and marked "not asked" — that is normal.

Tap **Submit**.

- **Online:** it sends within a second or two and you see a green tick.
- **Offline:** you see *"Saved — will sync when online"*. It is safely on your phone.

## If you are interrupted

Tap **Save draft** at any point. The interview waits on your Surveys screen under "In progress", exactly where you left it.

Even if you do not tap anything — your battery dies, the phone restarts, someone calls — **your answers are saved automatically after every question.** Nothing is lost.

## Working offline

You can interview all day with no signal. Every submission is stored on your phone.

The strip at the top of the screen tells you where you stand:

| It says | It means |
|---|---|
| ● Online · All work synced | Everything has reached the office |
| ⬆ Syncing 3 of 9… | Sending now |
| ⚠ Offline · 9 waiting | Saved on your phone; will send when you have signal |
| ⚠ 1 needs attention | Something needs a decision from you — open the Sync tab |

**You do not need to do anything to send your work.** The app sends automatically when signal returns, when you open the app, and every half minute while you are online.

---

## The Sync tab

Everything waiting to be sent, in one place.

**Needs attention** — something the app cannot decide on its own. Almost always a duplicate person:

```
Farming · Ramesh Patil
This person already exists in the office records.
[ Merge ]      [ Keep both ]      [ Delete this ]
```

- **Merge** — it is the same person. Your interview attaches to their existing record. **This is usually right.**
- **Keep both** — genuinely two different people who share a phone. It happens.
- **Delete this** — the interview was a mistake.

**Failed** — the office refused it, and the reason is shown. Tap **Retry** if you have fixed the cause, or **Delete** if it is not recoverable. Show the message to your supervisor if it does not make sense.

**Waiting** — normal. They will go when you have signal.

**Recently synced** — confirmation that your work arrived.

There is a **Sync now** button, which does nothing you need to do but is there when you want reassurance.

## Your work

The **My Work** tab shows every response you have submitted.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Submitted | Received at the office |
| Awaiting review | Your supervisor has not looked yet |
| Approved | Accepted |
| **Rejected** | Sent back — needs your attention |

A rejected response appears at the top with the reason. Tap it, tap **Edit and resubmit**, fix what was wrong, and submit again. It stays the *same* interview corrected, so it is not counted twice and your numbers stay right.

## Rules to work by

**Never uninstall the app while anything is waiting in Sync.** Uninstalling deletes the queue and the work in it cannot be recovered. The app warns you; take the warning seriously.

**Sync before you finish for the day**, if you possibly can. A phone that is lost or broken on Thursday takes Wednesday's work with it.

**Sign out only when you have to.** If someone else signs in on your phone, your unsent work is held safely but cannot be sent until you sign back in.

**Do not share your login.** Every response is recorded against the person who was signed in. Someone else's interviews appearing under your name affects your record.

**Keep storage free.** Photos add up. Sync regularly and the app clears them from your phone.

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A survey I was told about is not showing | Pull down to refresh with signal. If it is still missing, your supervisor has not assigned it to you yet. |
| "New version available" | Tap Update while you have signal. You can keep working meanwhile, but update before you go out. |
| The location will not settle | Go outside, away from buildings and trees. Wait ten to twenty seconds. If your survey allows it you can proceed with a poorer reading; if not, you must wait. |
| The camera will not open | Check Settings → Apps → permissions for camera. The app offers an "Open settings" button when this is the cause. |
| It says I am offline but I have signal | Some networks block data while showing bars. Try loading any web page. If nothing loads, it is the network, not the app. |
| Something has been waiting for days | Open Sync and tap Sync now while online. If it fails, read the message and show your supervisor. |
| The app closed unexpectedly | Reopen it. Your interview is saved and waiting under "In progress". |
| I cannot sign in | Check the email is exactly right. Use "Forgot password" — you need a connection for that. If it still fails, your account may have been deactivated; ask your supervisor. |

## A normal day

**Morning, with signal.** Open the app. Refresh. Confirm your surveys say "Form up to date". Confirm Sync is clear.

**Out in the field, no signal.** Interview after interview. Each one: phone number, consent, questions, photos, submit. The top strip counts up: *"Offline · 7 waiting"*. Ignore it — it is doing its job.

**Back in coverage.** The app syncs on its own. *"Syncing 7 of 7…"*, then *"All work synced ✓"*.

**Evening.** Glance at My Work. If anything was rejected, fix it now while the interview is fresh in your memory.

That is the whole job. The app handles the rest.
